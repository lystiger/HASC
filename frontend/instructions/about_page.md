ABOUT_PAGE_SPEC.md - Corporate Heritage & Trust (Scoped MVP)

1. Objective
Build institutional trust with a concise About page that highlights infrastructure, history, and compliance without duplicating the Contact page.

2. Scope (Phase 1 / MVP)
Only ship sections that can be supported with real assets on day one.
If an asset is not ready, reduce the section to a minimal stub rather than a full placeholder.

3. Page Sections (MVP)
A. Hero
- Visual: 1 high-quality wide image (HQ or warehouse).
- Copy: 1–2 short lines reinforcing scale and reliability.

B. Foundations Timeline
- Visual: simple vertical timeline (1px border).
- Content: 3–5 real milestones (year founded, certifications, expansions).
- Typography: use JetBrains Mono for years.

C. Infrastructure Gallery (Minimal)
- Layout: 2–4 images in a clean grid (not full masonry yet).
- Subject matter: warehouse, QC lab, logistics fleet.

D. Standards & Certifications
- Show 1–3 real certificate scans or thumbnails.
- Include issuer + year in text.
- If certificates are not ready: show a single “Compliance on request” card.

E. CTA (Link Only)
- Single call-to-action linking to `/contact`.
- No duplicate contact details on this page.

4. Design Notes
- Palette: Industrial Slate (#0F172A) + white for readability.
- Grid: 12-column alignment for text + imagery.
- Tone: evidence-first, minimal marketing language.

5. Out of Scope for MVP
- Full masonry gallery layout (upgrade later).
- Multi-language longform narrative.
- Heavy animations or complex motion.

6. Next Expansion (Phase 1.5)
- Convert gallery to masonry / “technical gallery” layout.
- Add richer history narrative blocks.
- Localized content (EN/VN) if translations are ready.
